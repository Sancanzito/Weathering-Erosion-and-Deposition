Stage 2 · Rock Lab B

💡 Hint: CaCO₃ + H₂O + CO₂ → Ca²⁺ + 2 HCO₃⁻
Limestone is CaCO₃. The more H⁺ (the lower the pH), the faster it reacts.

Goal: wear the limestone down until less than half of it remains.

Stage 3 · Rock Lab C

💡 Hint: 4 FeO + O₂ → 2 Fe₂O₃

Goal: change at least 70% of the iron minerals. What happens if one ingredient is missing?
